## Intro

I am a poet, a technician, a product manager, and an avid user of AI agents.

I am proficient in Java (Spring & Spring Boot), Qt, and Vue 3, and I make a living using them, but I don’t like them.

I am well-versed in Chinese culture, but I detest Confucianism; I am a laborer who believes in Maoism.

I am proficient in modern C++ and am passionate about it; I am familiar with and enjoy TypeScript; in addition, I am proficient in Python and am open to using it.

I am a passionate advocate of the Hexagonal Architecture and BDD/TDD.
